Michal Geyer

I’m a founding researcher at Dalet Labs, working on generative media.

After a few years training models for visual creation, I’m now interested in how generative media can transform the experience of consumption.

Previously, I was a researcher at Runway and a student researcher at Google DeepMind.

I earned my MSc at the Weizmann Institute of Science under the supervision of Professor Tali Dekel, and my BSc in Mathematics and Linguistics at Tel Aviv University.
